## Operational data not publicly established in this review

| Topic | Minimum useful information | Analysis enabled |
|---|---|---|
| Demand and service | Monthly product/customer demand, requested and actual delivery, cancellations, service targets | Implied uncertainty, forecast bias, OTIF and strategic fit |
| Inventory | Daily/weekly raw/WIP/finished stocks by grade, ownership, age, stockout history | Turns, safety stock, pooling and available-to-promise |
| Transport | Lane tonnes, mode, tariffs, lead-time distribution, dwell, Incoterms and damage | Landed cost, modal choice, pipeline stock and alternate routes |
| Production | Actual yield, bottleneck rates, changeovers, maintenance, energy limits, qualified routes | Effective capacity and aggregate allocation |
| Sourcing | Qualified vendors, shares, lead times, contract cover, quality and concentration by shared gateway | Supply risk and make-or-buy analysis |
| Copper economics | Actual TC/RC, payability, penalties, recovery, by-product contribution and settlement timing | Conversion margin and sourcing sensitivity |
| Coordination | Planning cadence, RACI, forecast ownership, transfer pricing, incentive weights and overrides | Five-category coordination diagnosis |
| Circularity | Return volumes/quality, scrap ownership, collection costs, yields and residue outputs | Closed-loop economics and net recovery |
| Finance | Consistent segment COGS, average stocks, receivables/payables and payment terms | Cash-to-cash and working-capital comparison |

These are questions for literature, instructor discussion or authorised interviews. They do not request disclosure of confidential information. If only ranges or anonymised examples can be shared, preserve that basis explicitly.
