# Hindalco supply-chain research collection

**Research cutoff and access date: 6 November 2026.** These are working research notes and linked datasets for SCM HIND PROJECT, not a finished project report or presentation. They cover Indian aluminium, Indian copper, and Novelis, which belongs within a group-level aluminium analysis. No claim is made that public disclosures reveal every supplier, facility, contract, shipment or internal planning rule.

The organising reference is **Sunil Chopra, _Supply Chain Management: Strategy, Planning and Operation_**. The seventh-edition publisher contents were checked [S01]; the full book was not accessed. Chapter mapping is provisional to that edition. Academic interpretations below are our analytical application of SCM concepts, not quotations or statements attributed to Hindalco.

## Traceability and use

Follow **analysis → E### evidence → S## source → URL and locator**. Source references such as [S03] refer to the source register, not to a numbered textbook citation. Tables also link facilities through F## identifiers and flows through L## identifiers. Citation keys remain stable when rows are sorted.

Keep four boundaries explicit: **Hindalco consolidated group; Indian aluminium; Indian copper; Novelis**. Standalone and India-business indicators can have different accounting perimeters. FY26 is 1 April 2025–31 March 2026; Q2 FY27 is July–September 2026; Q1 FY28 is April–June 2027. Dates on plans do not establish completion.

The evidence classes separate reported facts, policies, management assessments, historical cases, plans, original inference and gaps. “Reported” indicates attribution, not independent assurance. Empty CSV cells mean unavailable/not adopted, never zero. Do not silently replace a conflict with a convenient estimate.

Research prioritised company filings and operating disclosures, regulatory filings, certification bodies, government information, a technical conference source, and selected independent reporting. Full FY26 annual-report retrieval was blocked by file size; accessible annual sections were used. The 6 November India Q2 FY27 primary presentation remains an access gap; no unverified table was reconstructed from snippets. Recent Novelis filings were accessible. The bibliography records these differences.

For later group work, one useful allocation is Indian aluminium configuration; copper configuration/sourcing; and Novelis plus coordination/risk synthesis. This is a suggested division, not an assigned responsibility. Agree common terminology and source keys before combining sections.
