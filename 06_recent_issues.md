# Recent issues: research selection

The complete dated register is [16_recent_issues.csv](16_recent_issues.csv). It contains fourteen issues and explicitly separates events, current status, reported responses and suggested analysis. The research cutoff is **6 November 2026**. “Recent” includes 2025 events whose effects or responses continued into 2026.

For a later balanced project, the following cases provide complementary SCM mechanisms:

| Case | Why it is useful | Main concepts | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper concentrate/TC-RC pressure | Distinguishes feed availability from profitability | Sourcing; bargaining power; capacity/feed alignment | I01; I07; E031–E033; E065 |

| Digital alumina sales | Provides a current pricing/information-driver case | Competitive tenders; price discovery; CRM | I09; E051 |

For each selected issue use the same causal chain:

**Trigger → affected node/flow → operational consequence → cost/service/cash effect → reported response → residual uncertainty.**

Do not jump from a news headline to a recommendation. First establish the scope, date and physical mechanism. Then ask what quantity or comparison would test the proposed response. For example, “add safety stock” needs a specific material, location, service target, disruption duration and holding cost; it cannot remedy missing qualified capacity indefinitely.

S48 supplies regulatory context for CBAM, not a quantified Hindalco liability. S41 supplies scoped audit findings, not a conclusion that the entire network fails assurance. Market and regulatory claims should be refreshed before final submission.

Two late-cutoff gaps remain important: the fully verified India Q2 FY27 presentation and a dated confirmation of copper-recycling commissioning. Search-only leads are retained in the bibliography but not promoted into operating facts.
